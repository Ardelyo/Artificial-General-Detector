The Industrial Revolution was a period of major industrialization and innovation during the late 1700s and early 1800s. The Industrial Revolution began in Great Britain and quickly spread throughout the world. This era introduced the use of new energy sources, including coal and steam power. This shift completely transformed societal structures and economic practices. Furthermore, the transition to new manufacturing processes included the development of machine tools. It also led to the rise of the factory system. As factories grew, countless people migrated from rural areas to urban centers. Consequently, cities expanded rapidly to accommodate the new workforce. In conclusion, the Industrial Revolution remains one of the most significant turning points in global history.
