Yesterday, I was walking down the street—not just any street, mind you, but that weird one near the old wharf—when I saw a cat. A purple cat! Can you believe it? I stopped dead in my tracks. My heart was thumping like a drum. "Hey there, buddy," I whispered, but the cat just blinked with those glowing yellow eyes and vanished into a literal cloud of green smoke. POOF! Just like that. I rub my eyes. Was I dreaming? Did I skip breakfast? Maybe. But the smell of ozone in the air was definitely real. I stood there for ten minutes, just staring at a brick wall. People probably thought I was crazy, but honestly, in this city, talking to invisible purple cats is probably the most normal thing you could do on a Tuesday morning. I went and got a coffee. Extra sugar. I needed it. Row after row of gray buildings stared back at me as I walked towards the station, feeling like the world had shifted just a tiny bit to the left. Just a tiny bit.
